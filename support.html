FAQ

How do saves work?

Blackspire autosaves when you take the stairs, return to Emberhollow, and every few steps in the dungeon. You can also save by hand to one of three slots from the menu or at camp. Saves live on your device. For a manual backup, open Menu → Export / Import to copy a save code or file, and paste it back to restore it.

Does iCloud sync work?

In the iPhone and iPad app, saves are also copied to your own iCloud key-value storage when iCloud is available, so they follow you across devices signed in to the same Apple Account. On launch, the newest copy wins. If iCloud is off or unavailable, saves simply stay on the device. Nothing is sent to us; see the Privacy Policy. The web version keeps saves in your browser only, so clearing site data erases them. Export a save code first if you want to keep it.

Why is there no sound until I tap?

iOS and web browsers keep audio locked until you touch the screen. Tap anywhere once and the music, voices and sound effects start. If you still hear nothing, check the silent switch, your volume, and the music, effects and voice sliders under Menu → Settings.

What does Auto battle do?

During a fight, tap Auto and the AI plays every hero, your own character included, for the rest of the battle. Tap it again to take control back at your next turn. You can set each companion's style and spell habits in Tactics. Against weak monsters, the game may offer to auto-resolve the fight, and you can ask it to always do so in future.

How do I change the difficulty?

Open Menu → Settings → Game. Story: monsters have 70% HP and deal 60% damage. Normal: monsters as written in the System Reference Document. Hard: monsters have 135% HP and deal 130% damage. You can switch at any time, even mid-game.
