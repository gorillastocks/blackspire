Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

Blackspire collects no data. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information about you.

What Blackspire does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No tracking of any kind, across apps or websites.
  • No network requests of our own: the game runs entirely on your device and talks to no server of ours. The only exception is the optional one-time purchase in the iOS app, which Apple processes through the App Store; we never see your payment details, name or Apple Account.

Where your saves live

Your saved games and settings stay on your device. If you choose to use it, the iOS app can also copy your saves to your own iCloud key-value storage, so they follow you between your devices. That storage belongs to you and is managed by Apple under your Apple Account; we have no access to it and never receive it. You can turn this off in your device's iCloud settings. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes the local saves.

Children

Blackspire collects no data from anyone, including children. Because nothing is collected, there is no data from children to store, use or share.

Changes to this policy

If this ever changes, the new policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through the Support page.
