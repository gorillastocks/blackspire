Spoken dialogue

Voices generated with ElevenLabs text-to-speech on a paid Creator plan that includes commercial use, using voices from the ElevenLabs Voice Library. The Dungeon Master's read-aloud box uses the Voice Library voice "Raymond".

Sound effects

Battle and spell sound effects generated with ElevenLabs Sound Effects on the same paid plan (commercial use). Procedural WebAudio sounds remain as a fallback (MIT).

Dungeon and town ambience are original procedural audio.
